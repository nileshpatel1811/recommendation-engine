Gold Loan Match helps borrowers compare gold loan options based on what matters most to them. Instead of looking only at the advertised interest rate, you can consider factors such as loan amount, processing speed and whether you are taking a new loan or switching an existing gold loan.

How Gold Loan Match Works

Answer a few simple questions about your gold loan requirement. We use your answers to identify lenders that may be a better match for your situation.

The goal is not to tell every borrower that the same lender is the best. Different borrowers can have different priorities, such as lower interest cost, a higher eligible loan amount or faster processing.

What Should You Compare Before Taking a Gold Loan?

Interest rate is an important part of the cost of a gold loan, but it is not the only factor worth considering. Borrowers may also want to compare the loan amount they may be eligible for, processing time, repayment requirements, charges and the terms that apply if interest or payments are delayed.

The actual rate, loan-to-value eligibility, charges and approval terms can vary based on the lender, the borrower and the gold being pledged. Always confirm the current terms directly with the lender before taking a loan.

Banks vs. Gold Loan NBFCs

Banks and non-bank financial companies (NBFCs) can differ in their gold loan products, processes and customer experience. Banks may be attractive to borrowers who prioritise cost, while NBFCs may offer different processing or repayment features.

There is no single lender that is automatically the best choice for every borrower. The right option depends on your individual requirements and the terms available to you.
